the evidence each part needs, so research collects only what the report uses.

Writing rules: AGENTS.md §6 (plain language) and Russian business style. Every number traced to `docs/sources.md`
or to a stage-3 table. Mark proxies as such in the text («по данным аналогичных проектов»).

Open questions for the client that the report should state as assumptions:
- which buildings are protected and which will be demolished (from T08; if unclear, say so and recommend publishing the scheme);
- whether the company can publish the redevelopment scheme before March 2027 (no change to the project, only disclosure);
- who can act as spokespeople (company leadership, architect of the project, heritage specialists).
